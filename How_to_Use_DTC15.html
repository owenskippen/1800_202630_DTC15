Getting Started

Log in to use your personal features and save your settings.

  1. Click Login in the navigation bar.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Go to Home and choose a feature.
Login

Home

Home has the main tools, your recent scans, and the latest alerts.

You can choose Check a Link, Report a Scam, Alerts, or Safety Tips.

Go to Home

Scan a Lin

Use the scanner if you are unsure about a website link.

  1. Copy the website link.
  2. Open Scan a Link and paste it into the box.
  3. Click the scan button and read the result.

A safe result does not guarantee that a website is completely safe.

Login

Report a Scam

You can report a suspicious website or message

  1. Open the Report page.
  2. Enter the link and choose the type of scam.
  3. Describe what happened and submit your report.

Do not include passwords or private information.

Login

Breach Scanner

Check whether your email appears in known data breaches.

  1. Open the Breach Scanner.
  2. Enter an email address you own.
  3. Click Check Email and read the results.

If your account was affected, change its password and turn on two-factor authentication.

Check Email

Safety Tips

Read advice about scams, suspicious links, and protecting your accounts.

You can also share a tip when you are logged in.

  1. Open Safety Tips.
  2. Read the tips or enter a title and description.
  3. Click Add Tip to share your advice.
Read Safety Tips

Alerts

Read warnings about scams and data breaches on the Alerts page.

You can choose which alert types appear on Home from your Profile page.

View Alerts

Profile

Change your alert settings here.

  1. Log in and open Profile.
  2. Turn Scam Alerts or Breach Alerts on or off.
  3. Your changes save automatically.

Your saved choices will appear again when you return.

Open Profile

Stay Safe Online

Change your alert settings here.

  • Never share your passwords or verification codes.
  • Check website addresses before entering information.
  • Be careful with unexpected links and attachments.
  • Use different passwords for important accounts.
  • If something looks suspicious, ask someone you trust.
