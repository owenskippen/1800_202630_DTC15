Stay Safe Online

Here are some simple tips to help you avoid online scams.

Safety Tips

1. Check Links

Check the website address before opening a link. If you are not sure, do not click it.

2. Check the Sender

Be careful with emails or messages from people you don't know. Do not share personal information.

3. Don't Rush

Scammers may ask you to send money or act quickly. Take your time and check first.

4. Notice Security Warnings

If your browser warns you about an unsafe website, stop and leave the page.

5. Block and Report

If you receive a scam message, do not reply. Block the sender and report the message.

6. Ask for Help

If you are unsure about a link or message, ask someone you trust before taking action.

Need help?

If you think you have been scammed, stop responding and ask for help.
