Stay Safe Online

Here are some simple tips to help you avoid online scams.

Safety Tips

1. Check Links

Do not click links from people you don't know.

2. Protect Your Passwords

Never share your passwords with others.

3. check messages

Be careful with strange emails and text messages.

4. Protect Your Information

Do not share personal or banking information with strangers.

5. Take Your Time

Scammers may pressure you to act quickly.

6. Ask for Help

If something looks suspicious, ask someone you trust.

Need help?

If you think you have been scammed, stop responding and ask for help.
